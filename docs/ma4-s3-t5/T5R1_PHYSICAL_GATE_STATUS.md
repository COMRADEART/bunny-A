# T5R1 physical and distribution gates

- Physical microphone, speaker, acoustic echo cancellation, real-room barge-in,
  and audio-device reconnect: **BLOCKED**, no authorized physical session run.
- Android phone install, pairing, reconnection, and revocation: **BLOCKED**, no
  authorized physical device session run.
- Native installation, upgrade, uninstall, signing, and distribution: **BLOCKED**,
  no complete candidate or independent installation evidence.
- Narrator, TalkBack, mixed-DPI, and complete accessibility qualification:
  **BLOCKED**, no corresponding interaction session run.

An EV certificate is not universally required for Microsoft Store submission.
Direct-download signing and SmartScreen reputation are separate questions. No
store publication or public update was performed.
